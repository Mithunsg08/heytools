Your data stays yours, with clear limits

HeyTools has no accounts and no advertising, and works local-first. But some tools exist to talk to the internet (streaming, weather, speed tests, downloads). This page separates three things so you can judge each one: what never leaves your device, what is sent only when you use an online feature, and what is optional analytics.

1 · Never leaves your deviceYour notes, trackers, expenses, recordings, photos, passwords and settings.
2 · Sent only when you use an online featureSearches and links go straight from your device to the service that provides that feature.
3 · Optional analyticsAnonymous download counts. Off until you switch them on.

1. Information that never leaves your device

HeyTools has no server that receives your tool data and no account system, so we cannot read it, recover it or share it. This stays on the device:

  • Notes and to-dos, habit, water, focus, breathing, tally, timer and spin-wheel data, expense and bill-splitting records, Markdown drafts, and word-counter text.
  • Voice, video and screen recordings (Recorder) and Geo Camera photos.
  • Alarms, Anti-Theft Guard settings and its event history, theme, layout and other preferences.
  • Files you pick in the video, music, image, PDF, QR, hashing, converter, JSON and password tools. These tools process the file in your browser or app; the file is not uploaded.
  • Your data password, your fingerprint or face (Android keeps these; the app only learns "confirmed" or "not confirmed"), and the encryption keys.
How it is protected on the device. Private tool data is always encrypted (AES-256-GCM). You choose how it opens:
  • Password. A key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds) unlocks a random data key. Forget the password and nobody can open the data, including us. You can optionally add fingerprint or face unlock; it can only be turned on by entering the password, and typing the password never triggers the fingerprint prompt.
  • No password. The data is still encrypted, but the key is kept by Android Keystore (the app) or a non-extractable browser key on this device and the data opens automatically. This protects copied files and backups, not a phone that is unlocked in someone’s hand.
Backups you export are encrypted with their own password (AES-256-GCM) and need that password to restore. Not encrypted: video and music library files, alarm times, and general preferences. Encryption does not protect against malware running as you, a rooted phone that is unlocked, or someone who knows your password. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, the data is gone unless you made a backup.

2. Information sent only when you use an online feature

Nothing here is sent in the background. A request is made only when you open or use that feature. The service receives what it needs to answer (the search words, link, coordinates or file request), plus what any website receives: your IP address, your browser or app type and the time. Those services have their own privacy policies, and HeyTools does not control them.

3. Optional analytics and telemetry

HeyTools includes one optional counter, off by default. It starts only if you choose “Share anonymous download counts” (Settings or first run), and turning it off stops it immediately.

  • Sent when on: an event name (for example download_click or app_open), a device type such as “Android phone”, whether it came from the app or a browser, a random number made on your device, the page name, and a timestamp.
  • Never sent: your name, email, files, notes, searches, location or contacts.
  • Where it goes: a private script owned by the project maintainer (Google Apps Script), which keeps the counts. Google receives the request like any web host does, including your IP address.
  • There are no advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking and no crash-reporting service.

Notifications

Notifications are made locally by the app, not through a server. In Settings → Notifications you can switch off everything or one kind, set quiet hours, send a test, and check that your phone lets alarms ring on time.

Delete everything

Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data removes everything HeyTools holds on the device, cancels every reminder and alarm, and resets the app. Photos that Geo Camera already saved to your Gallery belong to the Gallery and are not removed. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.
