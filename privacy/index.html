Your data stays yours

HeyTools is a set of small tools that run on your phone or in your browser. This page says, in plain words, what the app can see, what stays on your device, and the few cases where something goes over the internet.

No accountsThere is no sign-up and no login to HeyTools.
No ads, no trackersNo advertising or third-party analytics code is inside the app.
On-device by defaultNotes, to-dos, habits, expenses, alarms, recordings and photos are stored on your device only.
You are in controlTurn notifications off, export a backup, or delete everything from Settings at any time.

What is stored on your device

Each tool keeps its data in your browser or app storage on the device: for example your notes and to-do lists, habit and water logs, expenses, alarm times, timer presets, theme and layout choices, and files you pick in the media tools. HeyTools has no server that receives this information, so we cannot read it, recover it or share it.

Because the data lives only on your device, if you uninstall the app or clear its storage, it is gone. Use Settings → Back up to save a file of your notes, to-dos, trackers and settings. The backup does not include videos, music, recordings, Geo Camera photos or your Spotify login.

Most tools work fully offline. These are the ones that contact other services, and only while you use that feature. The services receive your device’s IP address and the request itself, as any website would.

Update checks

The Android app can look at the project’s public GitHub release page to see whether a newer version exists. This sends a normal web request to GitHub (which can see your IP address). Turn it off in Settings → Privacy & data. Updates are never installed without you tapping the download. Android only accepts an update that is signed with the same key as the app you already have.

Anonymous download counts (optional)

If the project owner has switched on counting and you choose “Share anonymous download counts”, the app sends: an event name (for example download_click), your device type (such as “Android phone”), whether it came from the app or a browser, a random number made on your device, and the page name. It never sends your name, email, files, notes, location or contacts. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it off stops it immediately.

Notifications

Notifications are made locally by the app. In Settings → Notifications you can switch off everything, switch off one kind (alarms, reminders, timers, update alerts), set quiet hours for gentle reminders, send a test, and check that your phone lets alarms ring on time.

Delete everything

Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data removes everything HeyTools holds on the device, cancels every reminder and alarm, and resets the app. Photos that Geo Camera already saved to your Gallery belong to your phone’s Gallery and are not removed. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

Children

HeyTools does not ask for personal information and has no accounts or chat, so there is nothing to collect from anyone, including children.
