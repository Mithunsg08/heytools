HeyTools › About

Small tools. Big respect for your data.

HeyTools is a free collection of 44 everyday tools for your phone and browser, built around three ideas: no account, no advertising, and your data stays on your device.

What we are solving

  • App clutter. Players, recorders, notes, timers, converters and calculators usually come from different apps with different designs. HeyTools puts them in one place.
  • Sign-ups and ads for simple jobs. Counting words or resizing an image should not need an account or an ad network.
  • Uploads you cannot verify. Where possible the work happens on your device, so there is no upload step to trust.
  • Private notes and trackers. Your notes, expenses, habits and recordings are yours. In the Android app they are encrypted on the device.

How it works

Every tool is a self-contained page that runs in your browser. The Android app wraps the same tools, adds things a website cannot do (background playback, reliable alarms, notifications, Anti-Theft Guard) and encrypts private data. There is no HeyTools server that receives your content.

Local-first, with honest edges

Some tools are useful because they go online: music and video streaming, live TV, weather and place lookups, the internet speed test, media downloads. Those services receive the requests needed to answer, only when you use that feature. The privacy page separates what never leaves your device, what is sent when you use an online feature, and the optional anonymous download counts that are off by default.

Security, without overclaiming

HeyTools uses a Content Security Policy, blocks cleartext network traffic on Android, disables Android backup, keeps encryption keys in the Android Keystore, encrypts backups, audits dependencies and checks its own source automatically. These are safeguards, not a security certification or an independent audit. SECURITY.md explains what each one does and does not cover, including limits such as Anti-Theft Guard being unable to stop a hardware force-restart.

Who makes it

HeyTools is built and maintained by one developer, Mithun. The code is public on GitHub, where you can report a problem or suggest a tool.
