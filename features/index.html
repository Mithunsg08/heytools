Anti-Theft Guard

Locks the screen and sounds a loud alarm if your phone is snatched or unplugged. Android app only.

Android app only
  • Arms when the phone is charging and sounds a loud alarm if the charger is unplugged
  • Detects a real snatch (carried away, not just picked up and put down), locks the screen and sounds the siren
  • Alarm keeps going until you unlock the phone, or until your own time limit
  • History log of events, kept on the device

It cannot stop a hardware force-restart or the power menu; Android does not allow that without special powers.
